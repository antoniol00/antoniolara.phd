Instead of serving static decoy responses, GenPot uses fine-tuned language models to generate plausible answers to attacker requests on the fly, keeping adversaries engaged while their behaviour is logged. It ships as a Docker image and is part of the CiberIA research initiative (INCIBE, Project C079/23).

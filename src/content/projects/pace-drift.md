A reconstruction-error monitor over incoming traffic drives a dual Page-Hinkley trigger that fires before labelled feedback reveals a drop in accuracy. Across CIC-IDS2018 and UNSW-NB15, proactive triggering improves sustained F1 over reactive, error-based adaptation, at a cost below 0.5 ms per sample.

The University of Málaga celebrated the **International Year of Quantum Science and Technology (IYQ 2025)**, proclaimed by the United Nations on 7 June 2024, with a day of talks on quantum computing, communications and sensing, followed by a round table with national and international experts. Its aim was to bring quantum technologies closer to students and researchers.

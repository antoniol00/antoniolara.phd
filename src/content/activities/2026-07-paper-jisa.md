**Adversarial red teaming and imbalance correction in heterogeneous NIDS: a class-specific adaptive GAN framework** was published online in the *Journal of Information Security and Applications* (Elsevier, JCR Q2). It is joint work with Carmen Fernández-Gago and Jose A. Onieva, and the AMD-GAN code is open source.

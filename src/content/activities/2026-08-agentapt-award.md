The project **AgentAPT** was selected in the competitive call of the Chair of Cybersecurity of the University of Málaga and Google (VirusTotal). AgentAPT is an LLM agent with tool calling that interrogates Google Threat Intelligence / VirusTotal sandboxes to attribute malware samples to APT groups.
